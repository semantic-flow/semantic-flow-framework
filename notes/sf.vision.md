**Semantic meshes** will be the foundation for a new era of the semantic web, where people can easily mint their own expressive identifiers, manage and version their own data, and surf and incorporate other public data into theirs.

**Semantic Flow** is a framework for publishing resource identifiers (IRIs) and managing digital artifacts, especially knowledge graphs and other semantic data. By leveraging GitHub, Gitlab, and other static hosting services for storage and publishing, it enables a **dereferenceable, pseudo-immutable Semantic Web** where every IRI can return meaningful content.

Although meshes can just live in any filesystem or be published by any webserver, git infrastructure can provide a more-powerful storage medium that supports distribution, collaboration, and publication (through a variety of [[mesh storage topologies|wu.repository-options]].


## Benefits

- own your own self-describing data
- web-accessible version histories for digital artifacts when you want it
- customizable presentation for your identifiers
- reliable data persistence and referencing via an expressive DigitalArtifact model
- truly FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable)

## Features

- seamlessly integrate other data sources anywhere in your mesh
- generate and customize mini-sites or single-page applications for nodes in your mesh
